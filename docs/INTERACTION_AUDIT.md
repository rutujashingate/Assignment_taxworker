## Explicitly unimplemented interactions

There are no fake controls for production field verification/source exclusion, full tax corrections, dependency-level sign-offs, bulk undo, formal election/due-diligence/completeness decisions, amended e-file acceptance, next-year computation, real IRS filing, regulated signatures, automatic deployment or a personal video URL. Their gaps remain visible in Coverage and the requirement audit. The submission page reports deployment/video blockers rather than offering inert “Deploy” or “Watch video” buttons.

Normal boundary-disabled controls and preserved missing-reference links are intentional states. This audit does not claim exhaustive assistive-technology or independent CPA usability certification. Exact test results and any failures are in PHASE_8_VALIDATION.md.
